Why RAE SA Exists

Every animal deserves to be seen.

Project RAE SA began with a simple belief: an animal’s circumstances should never determine its worth. We are building a community-driven animal welfare initiative focused on rescue, protection, rehabilitation and creating better futures for animals across South Africa.

About Us

Who We Are

Project RAE SA is a growing community animal welfare initiative founded in South Africa. We are driven by a simple belief: every animal deserves care and protection.

Our Beginning

We started small, responding to the need for compassionate animal rescue and welfare support in our community. What began as a personal mission has grown into a movement.

Where We Are Now

We provide rescue assistance, welfare support, community education and are building partnerships across South Africa to scale impact.

Our Vision & Values

  • Compassion — We lead with empathy for all animals
  • Integrity — We operate with transparency and honesty
  • Community — We believe in the power of people working together
  • Sustainability — We build for lasting impact
  • Advocacy — We champion animal welfare

Join us in building something meaningful for animals in need.

Important Disclaimer

Submitting a report does not guarantee immediate rescue. For emergency situations involving injured or severely distressed animals, please contact your local veterinary emergency clinic, local animal control or SPCA, or emergency services if applicable.

Project RAE SA will review your report and respond as quickly as possible within our capacity.

Adoption Process

We carefully match animals with their forever homes. Our adoption process includes:

  1. Initial consultation to understand your household
  2. Health and background check on the animal
  3. Adoption agreement and responsibilities
  4. Post-adoption support and guidance
