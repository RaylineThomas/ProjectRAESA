About Us

Who We Are

Project RAE SA is a growing community animal welfare initiative founded in South Africa. We are driven by a simple belief: every animal deserves care, protection, and a chance at a better life.

Our Beginning

We started small, responding to the need for compassionate animal rescue and welfare support in our community. What began as a personal mission has grown into a dedicated movement of people who care about animals in need.

Where We Are Now

We are currently building our foundation as a growing initiative. Through rescue assistance, welfare support, and community education, we're making a real difference in the lives of animals across South Africa. We operate independently and in partnership with other organizations to extend our reach.

Our Vision

We are working toward establishing a fully registered animal rescue organization with a dedicated facility. This facility will serve as a hub for rescue operations, animal rehabilitation, community outreach, education programs, and sustainable partnerships across South Africa.

Our Values

  • Compassion: We lead with empathy for all animals
  • Integrity: We operate with transparency and honesty
  • Community: We believe in the power of people working together
  • Sustainability: We build for lasting impact
  • Advocacy: We champion animal welfare

Report an Animal in Need

Help us help animals that need our support

Upload a photo if available to help us better understand the situation.

⚠️ Important Disclaimer

Submitting a report does not guarantee immediate rescue. For emergency situations involving injured or severely distressed animals, please contact:

  • Your local veterinary emergency clinic
  • Local animal control or SPCA
  • Emergency services (if applicable)

Project RAE SA will review your report and respond as quickly as possible within our capacity.

Adoption Process

We carefully match animals with their forever homes. Our adoption process includes:

  1. Initial consultation to understand your household
  2. Health and background check on the animal
  3. Adoption agreement and responsibilities
  4. Post-adoption support and guidance
